Dataset v2 video scan, 2026-11-22: new episodes 200-399 (seeds 1204-1409), every frame, 3 cameras.
196,596 frames per camera, 589,788 in all. Read-only; per-frame values in data/eval/v2_video_scan.npz (local).

How (scripts/scan_video_glitches.py, glitch_regions.py, glitch_sheets.py):
- Decode with torchcodec as LeRobot does (checked: 0 pixel difference from LeRobot's own decoding), compare with the raw
  recorded images in 16x16-pixel blocks.
- "bias": a block whose colour is shifted one way (a wrong flat colour), unlike compression blur at edges and water
  dots, which goes both ways. Candidates: frames with a block over 6.0 (876 frames).
- Size of the shifted region: water-dot blur gives 1-5 neighbouring blocks (862 frames), the glitches 14-24 blocks.
- Raw check: in the same region, the raw frame against the average of its two neighbouring raw frames.
- Rendering check: raw frames that differ from both neighbours while the neighbours agree ("spike"). The top ones are
  fast motion at release (fingers opening, wrist camera moving); the raw images are clean.

Result: 11 single-frame encoding glitches, all wrist camera, all during the pour.
A rectangle in the top right of the view shows grey instead of the table. The raw frame is clean there (it differs
from its neighbours by 0.2-2.1 on 0-255, against 4.9-10.8 between decoded and raw).

Decision (2026-11-22): accepted as they are. Not re-encoded: the AV1 encoder is very likely deterministic, so the same

Limits:
- The faintest confirmed glitch scored 6.3, just over the 6.0 cut-off, so a few fainter ones may exist below it.
- Episodes 0-199 (the v1 episodes) were not scanned.
